I am a Senior Research and Development Researcher at <a href='https://www.kitware.com/'>Kitware</a> in computational imaging and computer vision. I obtained my PhD in electrical engineering at the <a href='https://cse.umn.edu/ece'>University of Minnesota ECE Department</a> under Dr. James Leger.

My primary research is in the domain of computational imaging, which is the intersection between optics, signal processing, and computer vision. My thesis was focused on creating noiseless and clutterless reconstructions in passive non-line-of-sight (NLOS) imaging using information beyond what is typically used for conventional imaging. At Kitware, my primary work has been in event-based imaging with neuromorphic cameras, specifically for space awareness and star tracking applications. Our Gitlab for these projects is under  <a href='https://gitlab.kitware.com/nest-public'>NEST Public</a>. I also have experience developing foundational models in underwater acoustics and developing GenAI pipelines to improve test and evaluation of models through the open-source toolkits <a href='https://github.com/Kitware/pybsm'>PyBSM</a> and <a href='https://github.com/Kitware/nrtk'>NRTK</a>.

I am open to new opportunities and collaborations, so please feel free to reach me at my email connor.hashemi@kitware.com
