Our workshop paper "Quantifying Accuracy of an Event-Based Star Tracker via Earth's Rotation" was presented at <a href="https://iccv.thecvf.com/">ICCV</a> conference in Hawaii. This work proposes a new way of evaluating event-based star trackers and shows that their overall accuracy is competitive with conventional state-of-the-art.
